# Future belongs to the builders

These are a few short sentences on what I think part of the future holds

Technological progress has been accelerating and will continue to do so.

The rate of acceleration is also accelerating and shows no signs of slowing down.

The cost and value of *having* an idea has been going down. 

Ideas are cheaper than ever. This trend should continue.
