# Future belongs to the builders

few short sentences on what I think part of the future holds.

technological progress has been accelerating and will continue to do so.

rate of acceleration is also accelerating and shows no signs of slowing down.

cost and value of *having* an idea has been going down for some time. 

ideas are cheaper than ever. This trend should continue. 

what matters in an ever improving world is *execution*. 

future is not just dreamers, but dreamers who can build. 

dreaming is cheap, building requires patience, focus and clarity. 

building requires iterations, each iteration improves the current build with function $f_i$ (today's technology and one's application of the technology bounded by one's skill), $x_{i+1} = f_i(x_i)$, one needs to be patient till $x_{i+1} - x_i$ is small enough. 

getting the function $f_i$ right for the first few iterations is crucial, this is a transferable skill and gains compound over time
